Executive Summary

JewelERP is an enterprise-grade Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform meticulously designed and engineered to address the intricate, high-precision demands of the jewelry industry. Unlike generic retail POS or inventory software, jewelry enterprise management involves physical weight conservation across gross, net, and fine pure metal weights, high-frequency price volatility governed by international bullion spot rates, loose and mounted gemstone grading (the 4Cs, sieve sizes, and millimeter dimensions), outsourced manufacturing through goldsmith artisans (Karigars) with strict metal loss/wastage tolerances, and statutory GST and Hallmarking compliance.

Mission-Critical Architecture: JewelERP combines a robust Laravel 11.x backend, a dynamic database-per-tenant multi-tenant architecture, multi-firm (branch) isolation, a real-time double-entry financial accounting engine, and an interactive drag-and-drop jewelry tag designer into a unified, high-performance operational cockpit.

Key Enterprise Capabilities

  • Dynamic Daily Metal Rates & Automated Stock Revaluation: Automatically updates the retail and fine value of all available barcoded inventory whenever morning market rates (24K, 22K, 18K, 14K, Silver, Platinum) fluctuate, eliminating manual repricing.
  • Physical Weight Physics & Loss Conservation: Enforces mathematical precision across Gross Weight, Net Weight, Fine Metal Weight, Stone/Diamond Carats, and Wastage/Tunch percentages down to three decimal places (0.001g).
  • Omnichannel POS Invoicing & Old Gold Exchange: Executes complex retail counter transactions including mounted diamond calculations, 6 distinct making charge models, old gold exchange deductions, multi-tier line discounts, split-tender payments, and instant tax invoice generation.
  • Artisan (Karigar) Metal Accounting: Features a dedicated Karigar Metal Ledger that tracks physical pure metal issued, finished pieces received, scrap metal returned, and actual vs. allowable wastage, settling labor charges in cash and metal dues in pure grams.
  • Wholesale Bulk Inward & Lot-to-Tag Conversion: Streamlines the intake of bulk jewelry lots (e.g., 1000g of gold chains) and facilitates rapid, error-free conversion into individually barcoded retail SKUs.
  • Autonomous Double-Entry Accounting: Employs an automated financial posting service that translates POS sales, purchases, old metal buybacks, and expenses into balanced debits and credits across assets, liabilities, equity, revenues, and expenses in real-time.

Chapter 1: System Architecture, Multi-Tenancy & Security

1.1 Architectural Philosophy & High-Level Stack

JewelERP is engineered on the modern Laravel 11.x framework running on PHP 8.2+ and MySQL 8.0+. The application uses a modular service-oriented architecture (SOA) where business-critical calculations (valuations, tag conversions, double-entry ledger postings, tenant provisioning) are encapsulated in dedicated service classes located in app/Services/ rather than bloated controllers.

Backend Technology Stack
  • Framework: Laravel 11.35.1 (Robust MVC, Eloquent ORM, Transactions)
  • Runtime Environment: PHP 8.2+ (Typed properties, Match expressions)
  • Database Engine: MySQL 8.0 / MariaDB (InnoDB, ACID transactions)
  • Dynamic UI: Livewire 3.5 & Blade Component Templates
  • Barcode Generation: Picqer Barcode & Milon Barcode Engines
  • Image Processing: Intervention Image 3.11 with GD Driver
  • Document Rendering: Barryvdh Laravel DomPDF & Headless Chromium
Enterprise Security & Compliance
  • Database Isolation: Isolated MySQL database per tenant schema
  • Multi-Branch Scoping: FirmMiddleware enforcing firm-level isolation
  • Role-Based Access: Granular permissions (SuperAdmin, Admin, Staff)
  • Audit Trails: Immutable InventoryTransaction & JournalEntry logs
  • Authentication: Guarded multi-auth (Admin guard, Web user guard)
  • Tax Compliance: Indian GST (CGST, SGST, IGST) & PAN threshold rules

1.2 Multi-Tenant Database Isolation Architecture

Security and data privacy are paramount in the jewelry industry where client databases, proprietary design catalogs, supplier rates, and financial balances represent immense financial value. JewelERP implements a true Database-per-Tenant architecture for its enterprise clients:

When a new jewelry merchant registers on the platform or is provisioned by the SuperAdmin, the TenantService executes the following automated workflow:

  1. Generates a dedicated database schema: invoice_admin_{admin_id}.
  2. Invokes DatabaseSwitcher::switch($dbName) to redirect Laravel's default connection dynamically.
  3. Runs isolated migrations (Artisan::call('migrate:fresh', ['--database' => 'mysql'])) and seeds default account groups, chart of accounts, purity definitions, and standard diamond/stone attributes.
  4. Establishes an initial default Firm record mapped to the merchant's trade name.
  5. Resets the central connection via DatabaseSwitcher::reset().
Runtime Database Switching: The TenantMiddleware intercepts every incoming HTTP request. If the authenticated user belongs to the admin guard and has an assigned db_name, the middleware executes DatabaseSwitcher::switch($admin->db_name). All subsequent Eloquent models, raw SQL queries, and transactions automatically execute against the tenant's private database.

1.3 Multi-Firm & Branch Scoping Engine

Jewelry businesses frequently operate multiple retail showrooms, a wholesale manufacturing unit, and a separate corporate office under a single parent entity. JewelERP incorporates first-class support for multiple Firms within each tenant database:

  • Firm Entity (Firm.php): Stores branch legal name, address, GSTIN, State Code, CIN, bank accounts, logo, and invoice letterheads.
  • Session-Based Context: When an administrator or staff member logs in, FirmMiddleware verifies whether a valid selected_firm_id is present in the session. If not, the user is redirected to the Firm Selector interface.
  • BelongsToFirm Trait: Eloquent models such as Product, PacketMaster, RetailPurchase, SellInvoice, and CustomerOrder utilize the BelongsToFirm trait, automatically scoping queries, foreign keys, and unique constraints (e.g. unique barcodes per firm).

Chapter 2: Master Data Setup & Valuation Engine

2.1 Daily Metal Rates Engine & Hallmark Standards

Precious metal pricing changes daily—and frequently multiple times per day—based on spot bullion trading on international exchanges (MCX, LBMA). JewelERP features a dynamic Metal Rate Master that serves as the price anchor for all inventory valuations, cost computations, and retail billing:

Metal Type Standard Karats Hallmark Fineness Purity Type Application in ERP
Gold 24 Karat 999 (99.9% Pure) Karat / Percent Raw bullion, coins, bars, pure metal conversion base.
Gold 22 Karat 916 (91.6% Pure) Karat / Percent Standard yellow gold jewelry, bridal ornaments, necklaces.
Gold 18 Karat 750 (75.0% Pure) Karat / Percent Diamond studded jewelry, rose gold, white gold designer rings.
Gold 14 Karat 585 (58.5% Pure) Karat / Percent Modern lightweight jewelry, daily wear diamond earrings.
Gold 9 Karat 375 (37.5% Pure) Karat / Percent Export-grade budget jewelry.
Silver Fine Silver / Sterling 999 / 925 (92.5%) Percent Silver utensils, pooja articles, sterling silver jewelry.
Platinum Pt 950 950 (95.0% Pure) Percent Platinum wedding bands, couple rings, luxury pendants.
Fine Metal Conversion Formula:
Fine Weight (grams) = Net Weight × (Purity Percentage / 100)
Example: An 18K ring with Net Weight = 10.000g → Fine Weight = 10.000 × (75.0 / 100) = 7.500g pure 24K gold.

2.2 Dynamic Product Valuation Service (Automated Market Revaluation)

In traditional legacy software, when the market price of gold surges (e.g. from ₹7,000/g to ₹7,250/g), staff must manually recalculate the selling price of thousands of tagged items or calculate prices manually during billing. JewelERP solves this via its autonomous ProductValuationService: 

// App\Services\ProductValuationService::updateProductValuations
public function updateProductValuations(MetalRate $metalRate)
{
    // Retrieve all 'available' unsold products bound to this metal rate
    $products = Product::where('metal_rate', $metalRate->id)
        ->where(function($query) {
            $query->where('availability', 'available')->orWhereNull('availability');
        })->get();

    foreach ($products as $product) {
        $this->recalculateProduct($product, $metalRate);
    }
}

Whenever an administrator updates the price per gram in the Metal Rates interface, the system initiates a background recalculation for every active item in inventory:

  1. Base Metal Value: Recomputes Gold Value = Fine Weight × Current Metal Rate.
  2. Making Charges: Dynamically recalculates making charges based on the product's configured making charge type (Percentage of Gold Value, Per Gram, Flat, etc.).
  3. Precious Attachments: Aggregates live diamond sums ($product->diamonds()->sum('diamond_final_price')), stone sums, and packet stone values.
  4. GST Recalculation: Updates subtotal and GST amount.
  5. Inventory & Pre-Code Sync: Updates the final_price on the Product record and cascades updates to the parent ItemProductData master.

2.3 Diamond & Gemstone Attribute Masters

Diamond jewelry valuation requires capturing the international 4C attributes along with traditional Indian sieve and millimeter gradings. The PacketAttributeController provides a unified, extensible architecture supporting eight attribute categories:

Diamond 4Cs & Geometry
  • Clarity: FL (Flawless), IF (Internally Flawless), VVS1, VVS2, VS1, VS2, SI1, SI2, I1, I2, I3.
  • Color: D, E, F (Colorless), G, H, I, J (Near Colorless), K-M (Faint), Fancy Yellow, Pink, Blue.
  • Cut: Ideal, Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
  • Shapes: Round Brilliant (RBC), Princess, Cushion, Oval, Emerald, Pear, Marquise, Heart, Radiant, Asscher.
Sieve, MM & Packet Types
  • Chalni (Sieve Size): Traditional Indian sizing plates: -2 (stars), +2 to -6 (melee), +6.5 to -11 (pointers), +11 to -14.
  • MM Dimensions: Calibrated sizes: 0.8mm, 1.0mm, 1.2mm, 1.5mm, 2.0mm, 3.0mm, 5.0mm.
  • Precious Stones: Natural Emerald, Ruby, Blue Sapphire, Yellow Sapphire, Tanzanite, Navratna gems.
  • Packet Types: Certified Solitaire, Loose Parcel, Melee Stars, Calibrated Gemstones.

2.4 Packet Master Engine (Loose Diamond & Parcel Tracking)

Jewelry manufacturers purchase diamonds in bulk parcels called Packets. The PacketMaster model tracks these parcels prior to mounting into finished ornaments:

  • Packet Identifier: Unique internal parcel code (e.g. PKT-DIA-VVS-001).
  • Attributes Binding: Foreign key links to Stone, Clarity, Color, Cut, Shape, MM, and Chalni.
  • Dual Pricing Matrix: Tracks rate_wholesale (cost/manufacturing rate per carat) and rate_retail (selling rate per carat).
  • Weight & Count Averages: Records average_pcs and average_wt (carats per stone) to detect stone loss or substitution during workshop operations.
  • Certification Number: Captures GIA, IGI, or SGL laboratory certificate numbers for solitaire parcels.

Chapter 3: Inventory & Jewelry Stock Lifecycle

3.1 Jewelry Product Identification: Pre-Code & Post-Code Schema

Jewelry retail demands a hybrid catalog structure: merchants have recurring design patterns (e.g., a specific peacock-motif ring), but every manufactured physical piece has unique physical weights, stone counts, and serial numbers. JewelERP solves this via a two-tier identification schema:

Tier 1: Pre-Code (Design Model / Series)

Stored in ItemProductData. Defines the design prototype:

  • Product Name: e.g., "Floral Diamond Solitaire Ring".
  • Pre-Code: Short alphanumeric design prefix (e.g., RNG-SOL).
  • Base Category & Subcategory: Rings → Solitaire Rings.
  • Standard Purity: 18K Yellow Gold (750).
  • Standard Making Charge & Wastage Rules.
Tier 2: Post-Code & Unique Barcode

Stored in Product. Represents the distinct physical piece:

  • Post-Code: Auto-incrementing serial number (e.g., 1001, 1002).
  • Full SKU: Pre-Code + Post-Code (e.g., RNG-SOL-1001).
  • Unique Barcode: Scannable Code128 / QR code.
  • Actual Physical Weights: Exact scale weights measured to 0.001g.
  • Specific Mounted Stones: Distinct diamond carats and certifications.

3.2 Physical Weight Physics in Jewelry

In precious metal inventory, standard unit counts (pieces) are insufficient; inventory valuation and loss auditing are strictly based on weight conservation:

Weight Classification Measurement Unit Physical Definition & Mathematical Calculation
Gross Weight (GW) Grams (0.001g precision) The total weight of the finished jewelry item placed on the certified scale, including gold, alloy, diamonds, enamel, wax, and gemstones.
Diamond Weight Carats (1 Carat = 0.200g) Total weight of all mounted diamonds. Converted to grams via: Weight (g) = Carats × 0.200.
Stone Weight Carats / Grams Total weight of colored gemstones, cubic zirconia, beads, or pearls mounted in the piece.
Net Weight (NW) Grams (0.001g precision) The actual weight of the gold/precious metal alloy:
Net Weight = Gross Weight - (Diamond Weight in g + Stone Weight in g).
Fine Weight (FN) Grams (0.001g precision) The weight of 100% pure 24K metal contained in the piece:
Fine Weight = Net Weight × (Purity % / 100).

3.3 Advanced Making Charge Engine (6 Calculation Models)

Making charges (craftsmanship labor) represent a significant portion of jewelry pricing. Different manufacturers, wholesalers, and retail categories use distinct pricing conventions. JewelERP provides six distinct making charge calculation algorithms implemented in ProductValuationService and SellInvoiceController:

Key Code Making Charge Model Mathematical Formula Typical Industry Use Case
val Flat Value Making Amount = Fixed Value Exclusive designer jewelry, antique heritage articles with fixed artisan price.
per_gld_val % of Gold Value Making Amount = (Gold Value × Rate %) / 100 Traditional retail jewelry stores (e.g. 12% or 15% making charge on gold).
per_pcs Per Piece Fixed Making Amount = Rate × Quantity Machine-made chains, plain rings, nose pins, silver coins.
per_gm_nw Per Gram on Net Weight Making Amount = Rate × Net Weight (g) Standard gold necklaces, bangles, where stones are excluded from labor.
per_gm_gw Per Gram on Gross Weight Making Amount = Rate × Gross Weight (g) Wholesale casting jewelry where labor is charged on the total mold weight.
per_gm_fine_wt Per Gram on Fine Weight Making Amount = Rate × Fine Weight (g) Bullion conversion, pure gold trading, institutional corporate orders.

3.4 Wastage (Loss / Tunch) Dynamics

During jewelry fabrication (melting, filing, polishing), gold particles are lost. Wholesalers and retailers account for this as Wastage % (also referred to as Tunch or Kada in Indian markets):

Wastage Weight (g) = Net Weight × (Wastage % / 100)
Wastage Charge (₹) = Wastage Weight × Current Metal Rate per Gram

In JewelERP, wastage can either be added as a financial surcharge on the invoice or factored into the pure gold conversion weight during wholesale settlements.

3.5 Wholesale Bulk Stock & Lot-to-Tag Conversion Engine

Retail jewelers regularly purchase bulk stock from wholesale manufacturing hubs (e.g., 500 grams of assorted gold chains in a single parcel). The retailer cannot sell this parcel as a single SKU; it must be divided into individual, tagged, barcoded retail products.

JewelERP handles this through the WholesaleStockController and TaggingService:

  1. Inward Lot Receipt: The parcel is entered as a WholesaleStockBatch with total gross weight, net weight, purity, supplier ID, and purchase rate.
  2. Lot-to-Tag Conversion Screen: The store operator opens the batch, places an individual chain on the digital scale, enters its gross weight (e.g., 14.250g), selects or auto-generates the Post-Code, and clicks Generate Tag.
  3. Atomic Balance Deduction: The system automatically creates a new Product record with status available, generates a unique barcode, and atomically decrements the batch's remaining balance:
    Batch Balance = Batch Balance - Item Gross Weight.
  4. Reconciliation Lock: WholesaleStockService::reconcileStock() prevents negative balance anomalies and ensures that total converted product weights plus remaining batch weight match the original supplier invoice exactly.

3.6 Inventory Transactions Audit Trail

Every gram of metal movement is permanently recorded in the immutable inventory_transactions table:

  • IN: Stock inward from Retail Purchase, Wholesale Lot conversion, or Karigar finished receipt.
  • OUT: Stock outward from POS Sell Invoice finalization.
  • RESERVED: Item locked in an unfinalized POS pending cart or customer custom order.
  • ADJUSTMENT: Authorized weight calibration or stock audit variance.
  • RETURN: Customer sales return or supplier purchase return.

JewelERP features a built-in visual Label Designer (LabelDesignerController.php, PrintTemplate, TemplateElement) that empowers jewelers to build custom label templates without writing code:

PrintTemplate Entity

  • Category Scoping: Assign distinct label layouts for Rings, Chains, Necklaces, or Bangles.
  • Printer Calibration: Settings for thermal transfer printers (TSC, Zebra, Citizen, Godex) with gap sensing and darkness controls.

Dynamic Drag-and-Drop Elements
  • Barcode Element: Code128, Code39, or 2D QR Code with configurable bar height and module width.
  • Dynamic Text Tokens: Product Name, SKU, Purity (22K 916), Gross Wt (GW: 14.500), Net Wt (NW: 12.200), Fine Wt.
  • Stone & Diamond Details: Dia Wt (0.45ct), Stone Wt, Color/Clarity (G-H/VVS).
  • Statutory Tokens: Hallmark logo, HSN code, MRP/Price, Certificate No.

4.3 Single & Bulk Tag Printing Flow

The printing engine (PrintController.php) supports two operational workflows:

  1. On-the-Fly Tag Printing: Triggered immediately upon saving a new product or converting a wholesale lot piece. The system renders the active template populated with product data and dispatches it directly to the local thermal label printer.
  2. Bulk Batch Tag Printing: Operators filter inventory by date added, category, or purchase batch, select multiple items via checkboxes, and generate a continuous printing stream formatted with exact label gap specifications.
  3. Audit Logging (PrintLog): Every print action records timestamp, user ID, product ID, template used, and reprint count to prevent duplicate tagging or unauthorized stock substitution.

Chapter 5: Sales POS & Invoicing (10-Step Detailed Flow)

5.1 End-to-End POS Transaction Architecture

The Point-of-Sale (POS) counter in a jewelry showroom is one of the most operationally demanding retail environments in commerce. A single bill may incorporate certified diamond rings, machine-made gold chains, custom bridal pieces with gemstone attachments, an old gold necklace trade-in with melting loss deductions, multi-tier promotional discounts on making charges, customer loyalty credit balances, and split-tender payment across cash, cards, and UPI.

5.2 Step-by-Step POS Invoicing Workflow Walkthrough

Step 1: Customer Identification & Statutory KYC Verification

The sales cashier begins by selecting an existing customer or rapidly creating a new walk-in profile:

  • Customer Master Lookup: Searches by Mobile Number, Customer ID, or Name via autocomplete.
  • Tax & Identity Compliance: For high-value jewelry transactions (under Indian Income Tax regulations, transactions exceeding ₹200,000 in cash or ₹500,000 total), the system validates the customer's Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Aadhaar/Passport identity.
  • GSTIN & State Verification: If the customer possesses a registered GSTIN, the system checks the state code to determine whether the sale qualifies for Intra-State GST (CGST + SGST) or Inter-State GST (IGST).
  • Live Ledger Balance Detection: The interface dynamically queries PaymentTransaction to determine if the customer has an unsettled credit balance (Udhaar) or an unutilized advance deposit.

Step 2: Product Addition (Barcode Scanning vs. Direct OTC Sale)

Items can be added to the invoice via two distinct workflows:

Workflow A: Barcode Scan / SKU Lookup

The cashier scans the physical jewelry tag using a handheld barcode scanner. The system matches the barcode against Product where availability = 'available', immediately populating the invoice row with pre-calibrated gross weight, net weight, fine weight, purity, making type, mounted diamonds, and stones.

Workflow B: Direct Non-Barcoded Item (Fast OTC)

Used for bullion coins, raw silver articles, or fast counter adjustments without pre-tagging. The operator enters Category, Net Weight, Metal Rate, and Making Charges directly using SellInvoiceController::addDirectSellItem.

Step 3: Dynamic Real-Time Item Pricing Engine

For each added item, the controller computes the gross line price through an exact multi-component hierarchy:

Item Line Total Formula:
Line Total = Base Metal Value + Wastage Charge + Making Charge + Diamond Total + Stone Total + Packet Total

Where:
• Base Metal Value = Net Weight (g) × Metal Rate per Gram
• Wastage Charge = (Net Weight × Wastage % / 100) × Metal Rate per Gram
• Making Charge = Evaluated per configured model (e.g., Per Gram NW × Rate, or % of Gold Value)
• Diamond Total = ∑ (Mounted Diamond Carats × Rate per Carat)
• Stone Total = ∑ (Mounted Gemstone Carats/Pcs × Rate)

Step 4: Old Gold & Precious Metal Exchange System

Jewelry consumers routinely trade in old ornaments to offset the purchase price of new jewelry. The SellExchangeItem and SellExchangeDiamond subsystems govern this workflow:

  • Old Ornament Assessment: The cashier enters Description (e.g. "Old 22K Bangle"), Gross Weight on scale (e.g. 24.500g), and applies a Melting / Dust Loss % (e.g. 2.0% for solder/wax).
  • Melting Fine Weight: The net melted weight is multiplied by tested purity to determine fine pure gold grams.
  • Exchange Valuation Credit: The net pure metal is multiplied by the prevailing Old Gold Buyback Rate to calculate total_exchange_amount.
  • Automatic Invoice Offset: The exchange credit is deducted directly from the invoice payable:
    Grand Total = Total Invoice Amount - Total Exchange Amount.

Step 5: Multi-Tier Granular Discount Engine

Unlike generic retail systems that offer only a flat invoice discount, jewelry retail involves complex negotiation across craftsmanship, precious stones, and metal wastage. JewelERP implements four isolated discount controls:

Discount Tier Target Component Calculation Logic
Making Charge Discount Craftsmanship Labor Deducted as a percentage (making_discount_percent) or fixed cash reduction (making_discount_amount) strictly against total making charges.
Diamond / Stone Discount Mounted Gemstones Percentage or cash reduction applied strictly against total diamond/stone charges.
Wastage Discount Metal Loss Surcharge Waiver or reduction on the computed metal wastage surcharge.
Special Overall Discount Taxable Subtotal Final bill discount percentage applied across the remaining taxable invoice value.

Step 6: Statutory GST Compliance & Mathematical Round-Off

Tax calculation in SellInvoiceController::finalize enforces Indian GST statutes:

  • Intra-State Supply: Applies CGST (1.5%) + SGST (1.5%) = 3.0% on Taxable Value (HSN 7113).
  • Inter-State Supply: Applies IGST (3.0%) on Taxable Value.
  • Round-Off Adjustment: Due to decimal splits across grams and percentages, the exact total may contain paise fractions (e.g. ₹124,562.43). The system calculates:
    Final Payable = round(Grand Total) and Round Off = Final Payable - Grand Total (e.g. -₹0.43), ensuring flawless currency settlement.

Step 7: Customer Udhaar (Credit) & Advance Deposit Settlement

The finalizing modal presents an automated settlement ledger:

  • Unutilized Advance Deposit: If the customer previously paid a cash advance (e.g. ₹20,000 for a custom order), the cashier checks the advance entry; this amount acts as a direct payment credit, reducing the remaining balance to collect.
  • Outstanding Udhaar (Prior Credit): If the customer owes ₹15,000 from a previous purchase, the cashier can settle that debit onto the current invoice, consolidating the customer's total outstanding obligation.

Step 8: Multi-Payment Split-Tender Settlement

Jewelry invoices frequently involve large sums paid across multiple payment instruments. The system records itemized rows in sell_invoice_payments:

  • cash: Physical cash drawer collection (monitored for tax threshold compliance).
  • cheque: Bank cheque details (Bank Name, Cheque Number, Clearance Date).
  • upi: Digital payment via UPI QR code / instant transfer with transaction reference ID.
  • card: Credit/Debit card POS terminal authorization with card last-4 digits.
  • Credit Balance (Udhaar): If Total Received < Final Payable, the remaining unpaid balance (amount_left) is automatically posted to Sundry Debtors against the customer's account.

Step 9: Atomic Inventory Depletion & Availability Lock

To prevent double-selling across multiple POS terminals, the product availability is updated inside a database transaction: 

// SellInvoiceController::addItem
$product->update(['availability' => 'sold']);

InventoryTransaction::create([
    'type' => 'OUT',
    'product_id' => $product->id,
    'quantity' => $request->quantity ?? 1,
    'gross_weight' => $request->gross_weight,
    'net_weight' => $request->net_weight,
    'final_fn_weight' => $request->final_fn_weight,
    'remarks' => 'Reserved via Invoice #' . $invoiceId,
    'admin_id' => Auth::id(),
    'sell_invoice_id' => $invoiceId,
]);

Step 10: Multi-Channel Invoice Generation & Delivery

Upon transaction commit, the invoice is rendered and distributed through multiple media:

  • A4 Tax Invoice: Formal legal tax invoice featuring company logo, hallmark accreditation number, HSN codes, detailed itemized metal and stone weights, terms & conditions, bank QR for payments, and digital signature.
  • A5 Compact Invoice: Optimized for rapid counter printers to conserve paper while meeting all legal requirements.
  • Thermal POS Receipt: Supported via cashreceipt-1.blade.php through cashreceipt-4.blade.php for high-speed 80mm thermal receipt printers.
  • Digital Delivery: Automated email dispatch of PDF invoices via MailjetService and instant WhatsApp / SMS delivery via Twilio API integration.

Chapter 6: Sales Returns, Refunds & Credit Notes

6.1 Sales Return Mechanics & Item Status

Customers may return purchased jewelry for exchange, resize, or refund. JewelERP provides item-level sales return tracking via is_returned flags on SellInvoiceItem:

  • Itemized Return Selection: Operators select the specific item(s) being returned rather than voiding the entire invoice.
  • Physical Inspection & Stock Disposition:
    • Restockable Condition: The item is returned to active inventory; its Product::availability is restored to 'available' and an InventoryTransaction of type RETURN is logged.
    • Scrap / Damaged Condition: The item is routed to the Old Metal Scrap bucket for subsequent refining or melting.

6.2 Financial Refunds & Credit Notes

Financial adjustments following a sales return are processed through CustomerTransactionController::refund:

  • Credit Note Issuance: Generates a legal Credit Note documenting original invoice reference, returned item details, and GST credit adjustments.
  • Customer Account Credit: The refund amount can be retained as a Customer Advance balance to be applied against future purchases.
  • Cash / Bank Payout: If paid out, a refund voucher is recorded, crediting Cash or Bank and debiting Customer Advance / Sales Return.

Chapter 7: Procurement & Retail Purchases (Cash & Metal Settlements)

7.1 Vendor & Supplier Management

7.2 Inward Purchase Invoice Creation (IP001...)

Retail purchases from manufacturers, bullion dealers, or wholesale casting houses are recorded in RetailPurchaseController::store:

7.3 Dual Settlement Architecture: Financial vs. Pure Metal Settlement

In bullion and wholesale jewelry markets, transactions between jewelers and manufacturers are frequently settled in physical metal rather than cash currency. JewelERP provides dual settlement modes:

Mode 1: Financial Settlement (Currency)

Standard commercial settlement via Cash, Bank Transfer (RTGS / NEFT), or Post-Dated Cheques recorded in retail_purchase_payments. Debits Stock in Hand / Purchases and Credits Cash / Bank / Sundry Creditors.

Mode 2: Metal Settlement (Pure Gold Grams)

The jeweler pays the supplier in physical 24K pure gold bars/bullion (e.g. paying 250.000 grams of fine gold). The system records pure metal grams transferred, debits Supplier Metal Account, and credits Bullion Inventory without cash movement.

7.4 Wholesale Lot Inward & Tagging Integration

Bulk purchases recorded in Retail Purchases can be directly channeled into WholesaleStock. A single purchase line for "500g 22K Assorted Casting Rings" immediately generates a wholesale batch ready for the Lot-to-Tag conversion process detailed in Chapter 3.

Chapter 8: Karigar (Goldsmith / Artisan) & Custom Orders Lifecycle

8.1 The Artisan Manufacturing Ecosystem in Jewelry

In fine jewelry, a significant portion of high-value inventory and bridal ornaments is fabricated by skilled artisanal goldsmiths known as Karigars. Unlike standard manufacturing where raw materials are consumed internally, jewelers issue high-purity gold bars, casting alloys, calibrated diamonds, and precious gemstones to external Karigar workshops.

The core operational challenge is Metal Accountability: every milligram of precious metal must be accounted for upon return, strictly regulating physical scrap and metal loss (wastage).

8.2 Customer Custom Order Intake (CustomerOrder)

When a patron commissions a bespoke ornament (e.g. custom bridal necklace or engagement ring), the transaction is recorded in CustomerOrderController::store:

  • Order Identification: Auto-generated job sequence: ORD-001, ORD-002.
  • Customer & Delivery Timeline: Customer ID, order creation date, promised delivery due date, and urgent priority flags.
  • Design Specifications & Visual Artifacts: Item Name, Metal Preference (22K Yellow, 18K Rose Gold, Platinum), Ring Size / Bangle Diameter, Estimated Gross Weight, and upload of design sketches, CAD renders, or reference photographs.
  • Order Advance Deposit:
    • Cash / Digital Advance: Advance currency deposit recorded via PaymentTransaction with status advance.
    • Metal Advance: Customer deposits old gold or bullion as advance metal, recorded with gross weight, melting loss %, and fine weight.

8.3 Karigar Job Card & Metal Assignment (KarigarAssignment)

When the workshop coordinator assigns the custom order to an artisan (CustomerOrderController::assignStore):

  1. Job Assignment Card: Generates an official tracking voucher (ASG-001) linking the Karigar ID to the Customer Order.
  2. Metal Weighing & Issue:
    • The coordinator places raw 24K pure gold granules, 22K alloy, or casting metal on a high-precision scale.
    • Records metal_issued_weight (e.g., 50.000g) and metal purity.
  3. Gemstone & Packet Issue: Issues specific loose diamonds and colored gemstones from PacketMaster parcels (exact carats, pieces, and certificate numbers).
  4. Contractual Terms: Agrees upon Making Labor Rate per gram (or per piece), Expected Completion Date, and Maximum Allowable Wastage % (e.g. 2.5%).
  5. Printed Assignment Voucher: Dispatches printAssignment slip containing detailed artisan instructions, CAD dimensions, and issued material weights.

8.4 Finished Receipt & Metal Reconciliation (KarigarReceipt)

Upon order completion, the Karigar delivers the finished ornament along with leftover metal remnants to the showroom workshop (CustomerOrderController::receiveStore):

Physical Verification Field Measurement (Example) Operational Reconciliation Function
Metal Issued (Initial) 50.000g (22K) Total raw metal originally dispatched to Karigar.
Finished Gross Weight 42.500g Total scale weight of the finished ornament.
Mounted Diamond / Stones 1.500g (7.50ct) Weight of diamonds/stones mounted into the piece.
Finished Net Weight 41.000g Actual precious metal contained in the finished piece:
42.500g - 1.500g = 41.000g.
Scrap Metal Returned 7.800g Unused gold sprues, runners, filings, and cuttings returned by artisan.
Total Accounted Metal 48.800g Finished Net Weight (41.000g) + Scrap Metal (7.800g) = 48.800g.
Actual Metal Loss (Wastage) 1.200g Metal lost in polishing, filing, and melting:
50.000g - 48.800g = 1.200g (2.4%).
Allowable Wastage Limit 1.250g (2.5%) Allowable loss benchmark. Since 1.200g ≤ 1.250g, full wastage is approved.
Artisan Making Labor Due ₹16,400 Craftsmanship fee payable to Karigar: 41.000g × ₹400/g.

8.5 The Karigar Metal Ledger (Pure Grams Audit Engine)

Unlike commercial trading software where vendor accounts are maintained solely in currency, the Karigar Ledger (KarigarController::ledger) tracks physical metal in grams alongside making labor balances:

Karigar Metal Ledger Balancing Rules:
• Metal Debit (Metal Out): Raw metal issued to Karigar → Increases Karigar's physical metal liability.
• Metal Credit (Metal In): Finished net weight + Scrap metal returned + Approved wastage → Decreases Karigar's physical metal liability.
• Net Metal Due: ∑(Metal Out) - ∑(Metal In) represents pure metal grams currently held by the artisan.
• Labor Dues: Making charges credited to Karigar's cash labor account upon receipt approval, settled via cash or bank payments.

9.4 Day Book & Real-Time Financial Statements

  • Day Book (DayBookController::index): Generates an immutable chronological register of all cash and bank inflows and outflows for any selected trading date, enabling store managers to reconcile physical cash drawers with recorded receipts at showroom closing.
  • General Ledger (AccountingController::ledger): Detailed audit trail of all posted debits, credits, running balances, and narrations for any selected account in the Chart of Accounts.
  • Trial Balance (AccountingController::trialBalance): Real-time summary verifying that total debits equal total credits across all asset, liability, equity, income, and expense accounts.
  • Profit & Loss Statement (P&L): Calculates Gross Profit (Sales Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold), Operating Expenses, and Net Profit for any custom reporting period.
  • Balance Sheet: Financial snapshot certifying that Total Assets = Total Liabilities + Owner Equity.
  • Accounting Resync Engine (syncAll): A fail-safe maintenance utility that can reconstruct the entire double-entry journal database from raw inventory transactions and invoices if historical data drift occurs.

13.3 Production Deployment Checklist (XAMPP / Apache / Nginx)

  1. PHP Extension Requirements: Verify that php-gd (for Intervention Image barcode scaling), php-intl, php-pdo_mysql, php-bcmath (for arbitrary-precision weight arithmetic), and php-zip are enabled in php.ini.

  4. Thermal Printer Integration: Install manufacturer-certified Windows spooler drivers (TSC Bartender, Zebra ZDesigner) and configure label dimensions matching the active PrintTemplate canvas (e.g. 68mm × 12mm).
